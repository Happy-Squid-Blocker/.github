Happy Squid is the world's first intelligent website blocker.

Traditional blockers are designed to block websites without exceptions. When you want to watch a lecture for class or find a recipe for your dinner, they won't let you. Happy Squid understands the difference between distractions and the pages you need.

When you’re setting up your blocks for YouTube, Reddit, Netflix, or any other site that distracts you too often, you can add your own exceptions for each site. Happy Squid makes sure you only visit pages of sites that match your own exceptions. This way you can block addictive sites while preserving your access to the pages you need and the content that’s worthwhile.

## Happy Squid's Features

* Block any website and entire groups and categories of websites.
* Add intelligent exceptions to blocked websites so you can still access the pages you need. Happy Squid makes sure the content you visit fits your exception.
* Explain to Happy Squid what you want to work on. Happy Squid blocks all unrelated websites and only allows the ones you need.
* Lock your blocked websites and tasks for a set duration.
* Schedule Free Time in advance and enjoy a break while keeping your blocks in place.
